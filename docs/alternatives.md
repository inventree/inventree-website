# Alternatives

InvenTree is not the only software for managing parts and inventory, and it may not be the right fit for everyone. Below are some alternatives that may suit your needs better, depending on what you are looking for.

## Parts and Inventory Management

- [Part-DB](https://github.com/Part-DB/Part-DB-server) - Open source (AGPL) inventory management, focused on electronic components
- [PartsBox](https://partsbox.com/) - Commercial hosted service for electronic parts, aimed at small and medium businesses
- [BOMIST](https://bomist.com/) - Commercial parts and BOM management, with a limited free version

## Asset Management

If you need to track equipment, devices or licences assigned to people (rather than parts and stock), an asset management system may be a better fit:

- [Snipe-IT](https://snipeitapp.com/) - Open source (AGPL) IT asset management

## ERP

If you need a full business system - including accounting, HR or CRM - consider an ERP:

- [Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/) - ERP with an open source community edition

## Spreadsheets

For a small number of parts, a spreadsheet may be all you need. InvenTree can [import data](https://docs.inventree.org/en/stable/concepts/data_import/) from spreadsheets when you outgrow it.

!!! info "Migrating to InvenTree"
    Moving from another system? The [PK2InvenTree](https://github.com/rgilham/PK2InvenTree) tool migrates data from [PartKeepr](https://github.com/partkeepr/PartKeepr) (which is no longer maintained), and other data can be imported from spreadsheets.
